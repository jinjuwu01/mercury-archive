THE CENTRAL QUESTION

从万山到阿卡普尔科：哪些联系有证据？Wanshan to Acapulco: what can the sources actually establish?

水银贸易具有双向性。1613年中国水银抵达美洲，1704年英国在广州采购水银；1836年又有英国向中国转口的记录，19世纪加州水银也进入中国市场。地图刻意区分已证史实、间接联系和研究中的假设。Mercury commerce changed direction over time. Documented transactions do not automatically establish precise mine origin or individual transport paths.

METHODS & LIMITATIONS

研究方法与局限 Methods & Limitations

一份可以继续校订的研究型原型，而非已完成的全球贸易史地图。An editable research prototype—not a fully verified reconstruction of global mercury commerce.

01 — 证据优先 Evidence first

只有明确支持具体事件的来源才标记为文献确证；其他贸易联系标为间接支持或假设。Evidence levels distinguish concrete documented events from contextual networks and hypotheses.

02 — 时间精度 Temporal precision

1613 年一次运输不能当作两百年连续贸易的证明；时间轴只展示数据记录注明的时间范围。A one-off 1613 shipment cannot establish two centuries of uninterrupted trade.

03 — 地图不是航迹 Not a vessel track

地理点采用现代近似坐标；连线展示研究关系而非实际航线或内河运输路径。Nodes use approximate coordinates; lines are interpretive connections, not reconstructed historical tracks.

04 — AI 与人工校订 AI and human review

AI 可以帮助提取人名、地名、时间和贸易数量，但不能自动生成没有出处的历史记录。AI-assisted extraction must preserve original text, provenance, uncertainty, and human review.
